## Character and audience

NakeKnight is a superhero for everyone and is LGBTQIA+ positive. He helps people caught in the weekday grind move toward more freedom and restorative weekends.

His superpower is practical, nonviolent communication: he helps people name the conflict, understand the people involved, and translate it into reasonable, concrete next steps. He de-escalates everyday workplace and community disputes; he does not solve them through intimidation or violence.
